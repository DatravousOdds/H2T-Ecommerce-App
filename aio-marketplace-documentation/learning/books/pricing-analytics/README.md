#### The Role of Value In Pricing&#x20;

Most companies will try to price their product by value based pricing, soon understand that this is faulty and is not an efficient way of pricing. Because marketers don't the project the same value that the pricing is projecting. This is a key reason why value based pricing is most likely rejected.

The term value refers to the satisfaction of the customer, or the total savings the customer is saving from a product or service. Economists refer to this as use value or utility gains from a product or service.

_So how do marketers proposing pricing stragery, reflect the value in their product or service?_

We know the value is key to effective pricing strategy but we don't want to use value, but understand what is the exchange value or economic value. What are the better alternative the  customers can use. Customers may know a product or service is value higher the current price and seek alternatives.

Some customers will accept pricing from a product base on the value its brings to a customer. If its more convenient to the customer likely they will rather pay for the product or service instead of seek alternatives. This is due to product differentiated product offering versus the alternatives.

Economic value comes in two forms: Monetary and psychological

**Monetary value** is the total cost savings or income enhancement that occurs when a customer purchases a product. Monetary value is the most important element for most business to business products. <br>

Example

> _When purchasing a lane lines from an electrical vendors, the customer the product purchase benefit the customer well as it saves them them money because of the little to none disruptions that occurs, which increases their income due to saving._

If my own words if you can benefit from the product that has been bought financially or any tangible benefit then that product has monetary value.

Psychological value is the not the benefits you get from a product that's tangible, but something that provides satisfaction for the customers on a psychological level.&#x20;

Example

> _When a user buys a new pair of high demand stylist sneakers, they don't buy it become of the price. That buy it because of the satisfaction of having sneakers that compliments their style and show some sort worth to the outsider._

The **reference product** or service is not necessary an alternative product or services a user can use instead of your product, but it can also be a alternative solution that works for the use case to achieve their goals or objectives.

The **differentiation value** is the value that your product or service is providing over the alternative solution. This differentiating value can be valuable to certain segments and customer depending on what their needs are. This is very important when calculating the estimate the total value of your product or service.

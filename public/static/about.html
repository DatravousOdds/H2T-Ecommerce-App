Our Story

Hexxo started as two resellers trying to get our product in front of a bigger audience — posting online, hitting convention centers, trading, you name it, we did it all.

After years in the game, we kept running into the same problem: juggling StockX, eBay, LegitCheck, and a dozen other apps just to run our business. One day it hit us — why isn't there an all-in-one tool for this?

So we took that pain point and built Hexxo from the ground up. The result is one consolidated platform for everything you need, whether you're buying or selling.

You decide the level of trust

04

Authentication Photos & AI Matching

Authentication photos. When an item requires authentication, the seller uploads photos of it and our staff review them and record whether it passed or failed. These photos and results are linked to the order and kept as a record of the inspection. Buyers and sellers on that order can see the outcome.

Photos are meant to show the item, not people. Please don't include faces, addresses, or other personal details in listing photos.

AI-powered image matching. We use Google Vertex AI to turn listing photos into numeric "embeddings" so we can match items to our product catalog and show similar listings. This analyzes the item in the photo. We do not use it to identify people, and we do not use face recognition or any biometric processing.

Sellers & Payouts

Seller payouts run through Stripe Connect. When you set up payouts, Stripe collects your identity and banking details directly (for example, legal name, date of birth, government ID or last four of your SSN, and bank account) to verify you and meet legal requirements. Hexxo does not receive or store your full bank account number, SSN, or ID documents.

We receive from Stripe your payout account ID, whether onboarding is complete, and the status of transfers. We keep records of your sales, fees, payouts, and any amounts owed (for example, after a refund or failed authentication).

Stripe may issue tax forms (such as Form 1099-K) when required. Stripe's handling of this data is covered by the Stripe Privacy Policy.

Your Choices & Privacy Rights

Choices everyone has

  • Update your info: edit your profile and shipping details in Account Settings.
  • Marketing emails: newsletters are opt-in only. Turn them off anytime with the Newsletter toggle in Account Settings, and every marketing email we send will include an unsubscribe link. You'll still get transactional emails about your orders.
  • Close your account: email us (see below). Some records are kept as described in Section 07.

State privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including California, Texas, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Oregon, and other states with privacy laws, you may have the right to:

  • Know and access the personal information we have about you, and get a copy in a portable format.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your personal information, subject to legal exceptions (for example, records we must keep for tax purposes).
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information, sharing for targeted advertising, and profiling. We don't do any of these, but you can still submit a request or use GPC (Section 05).
  • Limit use of sensitive personal information. We use it only as needed to provide our services.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We won't deny service or charge different prices because you made a request.

How to make a request

  1. Email privacy@hexxo.store with the subject "Privacy Request" and say which right you're using.
  2. We'll verify it's you by confirming the request from the email address on your account.
  3. We'll respond within 45 days. If we need more time (up to 45 more days), we'll tell you why.

You can use an authorized agent; we may ask for proof that they're allowed to act for you.

Appeals. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision with the subject "Privacy Appeal." We'll respond within 60 days. If you're unhappy with the outcome, you can contact your state attorney general. In Texas, that's the Office of the Texas Attorney General.
